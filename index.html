Why we exist

Climate data isn't climate intelligence

South Asia faces intensifying heatwaves, erratic rainfall, floods, droughts, and stronger storms. Global climate models are invaluable, but over this region they often show large biases and conflicting projections — too coarse for local planning, with systematic errors and disagreements between models on what's actually coming.

Rising risk

Millions of lives and livelihoods are at risk, and the decisions made today will shape regional resilience for decades. This isn't a distant scenario — it's a present planning problem.

A resolution gap

Coarse resolution, persistent biases, and contradictory projections mean raw global model output rarely translates into something a state planner, a farmer, or a hospital administrator can actually use.

Our answer

We turn climate science into regionally optimized, decision-relevant intelligence — through high-resolution modeling, AI and analytics, and outputs built for the decision at hand, not just the science behind it.

Two pillars

Where climate discovery meets climate foresight

Built on two pillars of excellence — advancing fundamental climate science, and transforming it into actionable foresight for climate action.

Climate Discovery

Question assumptions, decode complexity, advance understanding. We revisit accepted paradigms and test the foundational assumptions of climate science.

Climate Foresight

From science to strategy, from uncertainty to resilience. We transform climate science into actionable, regionally optimized intelligence for long-term planning.
