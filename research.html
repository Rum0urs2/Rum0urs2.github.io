Our research framework

From science to foresight.

A continuous journey that turns scientific understanding into intelligent foresight, enabling resilient futures.

  1. 1

    Understand the science

    We explore how the climate system works — revisiting theories, challenging assumptions, and generating new scientific knowledge.

  2. 2

    Generate climate information

    We develop and refine models and analytical frameworks that transform complex data into reliable, high-resolution climate information.

  3. 3

    Derive climate intelligence

    We convert information into actionable intelligence tailored to decision needs across sectors and communities.

  4. 4

    Enable strategic foresight

    We look ahead to anticipate change, evaluate risks and opportunities, and guide decisions for a sustainable future.

Pillar one

Climate Discovery — question assumptions, decode complexity, advance understanding

We challenge long-standing beliefs and explore the fundamental processes that shape Earth's climate system through theory, modeling, and data-driven discovery.

Question assumptions

We revisit accepted paradigms and test the foundational assumptions of climate science.

Decode complexity

We unravel the interactions and feedbacks within the climate system using advanced models and theory.

Advance understanding

We generate new insights into processes, dynamics, and extremes to advance the frontier of climate knowledge.

Drive scientific progress

Our discoveries refine models, improve predictions, and strengthen the scientific foundation for climate action.

Pillar two

Climate Foresight — from science to strategy, from uncertainty to resilience

We transform climate science into actionable, regionally optimized intelligence that empowers long-term planning, policy, adaptation, and resilient development.

  1. 1

    Long-term projections

    High-resolution, bias-aware projections of temperature, rainfall, and winds for 5–30+ year horizons.

  2. 2

    Risk & impact assessments

    Evaluate climate risks and potential impacts across sectors, regions, and communities.

  3. 3

    Decision support & policy tools

    Actionable intelligence and tools to support evidence-based policy and investment decisions.

  4. 4

    Development planning relevance

    Tailored climate insights for infrastructure, urban planning, water management, agriculture, and energy.

  5. 5

    Attribution investigations

    Assess the role of climate change in observed trends and extreme events to inform adaptation and resilience.

Need-based. Demand-driven. We co-create and deliver customized climate intelligence based on user needs and decision context.
