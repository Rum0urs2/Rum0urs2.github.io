02Our journey

From a global challenge to a centre of excellence.

01

The global challenge

The Global South, home to most of the world's people, faces greater vulnerability from poverty, inequality, and dependence on climate-sensitive sectors. Inconsistent projections undermine policy and adaptation.

02

India at a critical juncture

India aspires to a five-trillion-dollar economy by the late 2020s and ten trillion by 2032. That growth demands massive infrastructure investment — without costing us our climate.

03

The centre was established

In 2017, with support from the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India, the Centre of Excellence in Climate Modeling was established at IIT Delhi.

04

Our evolution

Our scope has widened. Today we are the Centre of Excellence for Climate Information — generating actionable intelligence while advancing understanding of the climate system.

05

Our promise

Science-based, reliable, and actionable climate information that empowers decision-makers and builds a resilient, sustainable future for all.
