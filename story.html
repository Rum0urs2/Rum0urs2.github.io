Our journey

Five steps, one mission

  1. 1

    The global challenge

    The Global South, home to the majority of the world's population, faces greater vulnerability due to poverty, inequality, and dependence on climate-sensitive sectors. Inconsistent climate projections in many vulnerable regions undermine effective policy-making and adaptation.

  2. 2

    India at a critical juncture

    As a young and developing nation, India aspires to become a five-trillion-dollar economy by the late 2020s and a ten-trillion-dollar economy by 2032. This growth demands transformative reforms and massive infrastructure investment — but it must not come at the cost of our climate.

  3. 3

    The Centre was established

    In 2017, with support from the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India, the Centre of Excellence in Climate Modeling was established at IIT Delhi, envisioned to address critical climate challenges facing India and the surrounding region.

  4. 4

    Our evolution

    Over the years, our scope has expanded. Today, we are a Centre of Excellence for Climate Information — generating actionable intelligence to combat climate change while advancing understanding of the complex climate system.

  5. 5

    Our promise

    We are committed to delivering science-based, reliable, and actionable climate information that empowers decision-makers and builds a climate-resilient, sustainable future for all.
